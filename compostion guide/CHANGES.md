- 42° -> 34° desktop FOV for a premium product-photography composition.
- Camera raised from `y=0.24` to `y=0.78` and explicitly aimed at the product group.
- Globe pushed from `z=-1.2` to `z=-1.85` and raised.
- Phone moved much farther toward camera to create a true foreground layer.
- Tablet scale reduced from `0.84` to `0.76` and yaw increased for visible edge thickness.
- Laptop yaw increased and camera elevation now exposes keyboard/deck depth.
- Platform footprint reduced substantially.
- Neon ring and underglow reduced so the devices, not the red ring, become the focal point.
- Device material changed from almost-black 92% metal to a lighter physical titanium material.
- Screen materials now use subtle glass/clearcoat rather than completely flat basic materials.
- Shadow maps enabled; key light casts soft shadows.
- Added separate blue/white and red edge lights to outline black chassis against the dark hero.
- Mobile responsiveness now scales the actual scene as well as changing camera FOV/distance.
